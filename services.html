Our services

Flexible office, shipping, and basic technology support for small businesses. Service costs are discussed and agreed upon before work begins.

▤

Office Administrative Services

General Labor

Standard services such as organization, filing, or other office tasks are billed at a contracted rate.

Additional Services

Additional tasks, such as preparing letters, are billed at a broken-down cost based on materials purchased and used.

Shipping Services

We now have a label printer for shipping. If you need something like an account review book sent to your client, we can prepare it and mail it directly, cutting out the middle step. Packaging fees may apply. Shipping fees are billed directly back to the client with no upcharge.

⌘

Basic IT Services

Help with connection, software, or system errors on computers, tablets, printers, or mobile devices.

Hourly Rate

If a problem takes more than two hours to resolve, an hourly rate applies.

Flat Rate

A flat rate applies to problems solved in under two hours or that cannot be fixed.

House or Business Call

House calls are available. A fuel surcharge may apply in addition to the service rate, depending on travel time to the location.

◷

Bookkeeping Services

Bookkeeping is not currently available. It will be added after training and certification are completed.

Hourly Rate

The hourly rate applies to services needed. Hours per week and monthly rates are negotiable.
